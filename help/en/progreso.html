Download progress window

It opens when a download starts (according to Options > Downloads > Customize "Download progress" window).

  • Download status: address, status, size, downloaded, transfer rate, time left and whether it can be resumed. Show details shows each connection's position and the connection table (N., Downloaded, Info).
  • Speed limiter: a limit just for this download, in KBytes/sec, and whether to remember it when stopping and resuming.
  • Options on completion: show the download complete dialog, exit IDMClone, or shut down, sleep or hibernate the computer when done, and force processes to terminate. Exit and Turn off can only be checked with "Show download complete dialog" unchecked. They apply to this download while IDMClone stays open.
  • Hide tab removes that tab from this window and from the next ones, as in IDM. To get it back, check it in Options > Downloads > Customize the download progress window.
  • Pause stops the download (it can be resumed later) and Cancel stops it and closes the window; the download stays in the list.

If the download limit from Options > Connection is reached, the status says until what time it waits.
