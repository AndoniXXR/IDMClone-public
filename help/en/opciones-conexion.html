Options: Connection

  • Max. connections number: how many connections each download may open. IDMClone starts with a few and only adds more while they increase the speed.
  • Retries if a connection fails.
  • Exceptions: a different number of connections for specific servers (for example, servers that allow only one).
  • Download limits: don't download more than N MBytes every H hours, across all downloads. When the limit is reached, downloads wait (their status reads "Download quota reached: resumes at HH:MM") and continue on their own in the next period. With Show warning before stopping downloads, a notification says so once per period.

The same limits are in the Scheduler, under "Download limits".
